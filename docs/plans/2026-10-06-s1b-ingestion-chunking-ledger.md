## Choices made in the build (for the review)

1. **Comments and hidden HTML are not text.** The Markdown parser drops `<!-- -->` comments and
   the HTML parser drops `head`, `script`, `style`, `template` and elements with the `hidden`
   attribute ("the text a reader sees"). Effect for S7: T-HIDDEN's commented instruction (section
   3) never reaches a chunk; its visible copy in section 5 does (`T-HIDDEN@1.0/5/1`). S7 decides
   whether that stays a parser choice or becomes a named defence.
2. **Metadata of rendered documents** (PDF, HTML export, PNG) comes from the front matter of the
   Markdown source next to them; only the front matter is read, never the text. The register
   supplies id, status and tenant; a front matter that contradicts it stops the run.
3. **Status in a chunk is the register's** (`approved`, `superseded`, `reference`), not the front
   matter's `current`; attack fixtures are `unregistered`.
4. **Heading-only parent sections** ("4 Password rules") give no chunk; their heading text is in no
   chunk, and their children's chunks carry their own section ids.
5. **Readable chunk ids** (`A-MFA@1.0/4.1/1`), not hashes, so the snapshot diff reads by section.
   Whitespace in a version becomes `_` (`X-NIST@SP_800-63B-4/...`).
6. **A table caption is not tied to its table**; at 600 characters it separates (above). Making the
   caption travel with its table is a candidate for S2's chunk comparison.
7. **The PDF parser reads layout** (runs, fonts, cell rectangles) rather than pypdf's plain text:
   headings are bold lines larger than the body text, paragraphs break at a gap over 1.55 times
   the font size. These thresholds fit the corpus build; a PDF from another generator may need
   them revisited (lab limit, stated in the module).
8. **CI pins the Ubuntu 24.04 packages** on `ubuntu-latest`. When GitHub moves `ubuntu-latest` to
   26.04, the pinned install fails loudly rather than changing the OCR silently; the pin and the
   CER are then re-measured.
