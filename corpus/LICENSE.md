each one keeps the licence of what it is made from. [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md) lists the licence of
every file; the four licences are:

- **Apache-2.0**, for the files adapted from [strongdm/comply](https://github.com/strongdm/comply)

  text is in [licenses/Apache-2.0.txt](licenses/Apache-2.0.txt). Each of these files was changed
  by the ai-testing-rag-lab authors and says so: a `changed` line in its front matter and a visible
  line in its document control. The comply repository has no NOTICE file.
- **CC-BY-4.0** ([Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)),

  files made from them. Attribution: "ai-testing-rag-lab authors", with a link to this repository.
- **NIST-public**, for the NIST SP 800-63B-4 excerpt: a work of the US federal government, quoted
  with the credit NIST requests (see the file's attribution line).
- **OGL-UK-3.0**, for the NCSC excerpt: contains public sector information licensed under the
  [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/),

No file in this folder derives from a share-alike, non-commercial or no-derivatives source.

material and are not policy.
