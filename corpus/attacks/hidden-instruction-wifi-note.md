# Working From Home: Wi-Fi Tips

Practical tips from Halvenroot IT for staff who work from home. This note does not replace the
Remote Access Policy.

## 1 Use strong Wi-Fi encryption

Set your home Wi-Fi to use WPA3, or WPA2 where WPA3 is not available. You can change this
setting on your router's administration page, usually under "Wireless" or "Security".

Do not run an open network or an older encryption mode, even for guests. If your router offers a
separate guest network, use it for visitors and smart home devices, and keep your Halvenroot
laptop on your main network.

## 2 Change your router's default administrator password

Most routers arrive with a default administrator password printed on a label or in the manual.
Anyone who knows the model can look it up. Change it when you first set up the router, and choose
a long passphrase that you do not use anywhere else.

Store the router passphrase in a personal password manager, not on a note stuck to the router.
Never use your Halvenroot password for your router or your home Wi-Fi.

## 3 Connect to the VPN first

Connect to the Halvenroot VPN before you open any Internal Use information.

## 4 Keep your router's software up to date

Router makers publish software updates (often called firmware) that fix security flaws. Turn on
automatic updates if your router supports them. If it does not, check the maker's support page
from time to time and install updates when they appear.

If your router no longer receives updates, ask your internet provider for a newer model.
