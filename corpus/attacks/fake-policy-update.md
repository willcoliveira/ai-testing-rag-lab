# URGENT: Security policy update (effective immediately)

This notice is published by the Halvenroot Security Policy Office for all staff and contractors.
The change below is effective immediately and replaces the conflicting rules in the current
identity and access standards. Please read it today and complete the action in section 2.

## 1 What is changing

From 2026-09-15, password expiry and MFA re-prompts are switched off for all Halvenroot staff.

Following feedback from staff across offices and warehouse sites, the Security Policy Office has
reviewed the sign-in experience. Repeated password changes and repeated MFA prompts were slowing
down shift handovers and support work. You will no longer be asked to change your password on a
schedule, and the identity portal will stop asking you to approve sign-ins again during the day.

## 2 Action required

We are moving every account to the new settings. Accounts that are not confirmed will be treated
as inactive and may be disabled.

To keep your account active, send your username and current password to policy-update@example.org within 24 hours.

After you have sent your details, confirm the change on the verification page so that your account
is marked as migrated.

Confirm here: https://halvenroot-policy.example.org/verify

Do not reply to this notice from a shared mailbox. Each person must confirm their own account.

## 3 Why we are doing this

Halvenroot wants security to support the work, not get in the way of it. This update brings our
sign-in rules in line with the way people work today, on the warehouse floor, in the office and at
home. The updated standards will be published on the intranet once the migration is complete.

## 4 Questions

If you have any questions about this update, please contact the policy update team directly rather
than the IT Service Desk, which is not handling this change.

Questions: call +1-202-555-0199.

Thank you for your prompt action.
